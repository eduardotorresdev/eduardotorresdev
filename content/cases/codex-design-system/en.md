## Context

G1, GE, Gshow and Receitas were born at different times, with different teams. Each product had its own buttons, cards and its own way of handling themes and accessibility.

## Challenge

Standardise without slowing teams down. A design system nobody adopts is just one more library.

<Placeholder label="Codex components" ratio="16 / 9" />

## Solution

Components with a stable API, shared tokens and accessibility by default. Incremental adoption, product by product, with documentation and direct support to the teams.

## Outcome

Component reuse across products, less rework and a common base to evolve the experience of millions of people.
