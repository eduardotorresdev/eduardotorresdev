## Context

Live coverage is the format with the highest audience at peak moments: elections, the World Cup, breaking news. Millions of people follow the same page at the same time, on phones with every kind of connection.

<Placeholder label="Live coverage screenshot" />

## Challenge

The format had to increase content consumption without making the page heavier or breaking real-time reading. Every interaction change had a direct impact on revenue and on Core Web Vitals.

## Solution

New interaction patterns, measured with A/B and MAB tests before becoming the default. Components shared between G1 and GE, with care for progressive loading and behaviour under load.
